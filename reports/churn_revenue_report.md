# Executive summary
The analysis of monthly metrics from January to December reveals a declining trend in active and paid users, alongside increasing churn rates in the latter months. Revenue has also decreased, indicating potential issues with user retention and satisfaction.

# Monthly revenue trend
Monthly revenue has shown a consistent decline from January's $15,710.2 to December's $6,166.14. The most significant drop occurred between months 1 and 2, and the trend continued downward, with December reflecting the lowest revenue of the year.

# Churn trend
Churn rates have increased notably, starting from 0% in January to 11.53% in December. The churn rate peaked in months 11 and 12, with rates of 10.34% and 11.53%, respectively, indicating a growing concern for user retention.

# ARPU trend
Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) has remained relatively stable, fluctuating between $15.33 and $15.71 throughout the year. However, a slight downward trend is observed towards the end of the year, with ARPU reaching $15.45 in December.

# Data quality checks
All data quality checks have passed, confirming the integrity of the dataset. However, there are two anomalies: 
- Month 11 has a high churn rate of 10.34%, which is above the threshold.
- Month 12 has an even higher churn rate of 11.53%, also exceeding the threshold.

# Business interpretation
The declining trend in active and paid users, coupled with rising churn rates, suggests that customer satisfaction may be deteriorating, necessitating immediate attention to user experience and retention strategies. The significant drop in revenue indicates that the business may need to explore new pricing strategies or value propositions to attract and retain users. Addressing the high churn rates in the last two months should be a priority to stabilize the user base and revenue moving forward.
