# Executive summary
The analysis of monthly metrics reveals a declining trend in active and paid users over the year, alongside increasing churn rates, particularly in the last two months. Monthly revenue has also decreased, reflecting the drop in user engagement.

# Monthly revenue trend
Monthly revenue has shown a consistent decline from $15,710.2 in Month 1 to $6,166.14 in Month 12. The most significant drop occurred between Month 1 and Month 2, with revenue decreasing by approximately $849.5, and continued to decline steadily thereafter.

# Churn trend
Churn rates have increased over the year, starting from 0% in Month 1 to 11.53% in Month 12. Notably, Months 11 and 12 experienced high churn rates of 10.34% and 11.53%, respectively, indicating a concerning trend that requires immediate attention.

# ARPU trend
Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) has remained relatively stable, fluctuating between $15.33 and $15.71 throughout the year. However, a slight decrease in ARPU was observed in the last few months, with Month 12 recording an ARPU of $15.45.

# Data quality checks
All data quality checks have passed, confirming the integrity of the dataset. However, there are anomalies related to high churn rates in Months 11 and 12, which exceeded the acceptable threshold.

# Business interpretation
The declining trend in active and paid users, coupled with rising churn rates, suggests potential issues with user retention and satisfaction. The significant drop in monthly revenue highlights the urgency for strategic interventions to enhance user engagement and reduce churn. Addressing the factors contributing to the high churn rates in the last two months is critical to stabilizing revenue and improving overall business performance.
